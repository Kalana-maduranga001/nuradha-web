LOADING PRODUCT

Model

AD24032021

Description

This hauler has been created with a “V” shaped drum with spokes and sheaves. The drum of this wincher has the ability to pull different varieties of commercial fishing nets for sale and sheaves to the intended pull line or rope. Its control valve allows it to gauge and control the speed and since the design has utilized advanced technology, this hauler is able to pull a high load.
The hauler is manufactured with marine-grade aluminum, and stainless steel frame along with brackets to suit the fishing industry requirements. It includes an upper hauling unit, a hydraulic motor with a control valve, hydraulic hoses, an oil tank, and a pump unit with brackets. This can help any global fishing company with its custom requirements.
Specifications
  • Hydraulic Motor - 200cc/rev
  • Pull - Net side 300Kg / Line side 150Kg
  • Drum & Sleeve Diameter - 400mm
  • Oil flow - 27 L/min
  • Body Materials - Marine Grade Aluminum , Stainless steel & Galvanized parts.
  • Speed - 70 M/min
  • Gross Weight - 160Kg
  • Line thickness - 4-14mm
  • Pump - 30 cc/rev (Pump will be driven by a coupling that connects to the vessel's main engine.)
