Model

OL 12012020

Description

In the commercial fishing industry, this hauler is utilized for light and medium-scale commercial fishing, and heavy leisure fishing. The hauler’s heavy structure is built using brass and stainless steel. It comprises an 82” tall stainless steel arm and the hauling units offer flexibility to adjust the height. This unit entails an automatic press arm, ejector knife, heavy-duty pulley, hydraulic motor with a control valve, hydraulic hoses with s/s fittings, oil tank, and a hydraulic pump unit along with brackets. 
Specifications
  • Hauling power - 425Kg
  • Speed - 0-70 m/min
  • Oil Flow - 27 L/min
  • Sleeves Diameter - 35 cm
  • Body Materials - Stainless steel, Brass, Galvanized brackets
  • Max Pressure - 140 Bar
  • Gross Weight - 165Kg
  • Rope Sizes - 4-16mm
  • Pump Unit - 30 cc/rev (Pump will be driven by a coupling that connects to the vessel's main engine.)
