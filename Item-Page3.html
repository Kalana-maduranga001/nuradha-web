Model

AM28072022

Description

Our commercial fishing supplies offer this hauler which has a “W” shaped aluminum drum and its covered with high-quality rubber layers. The net can be conveniently pulled using the rubber surface. Generally, in Sri Lanka amongst commercial fishing nets, this net is used in catching Skipjack Tuna (Balaya) fish, yet it can be used with any other commercial fishing nets for sale as well. This is also available with Port or Starboard models. If you are a global fishing company or a local player this fishing hauler can assist you.
This hauler is manufactured with a marine-grade aluminum structure with a heavy stainless-steel frame with brackets. It includes an upper hauling unit, control valve, Hydraulic hoses, oil tank, and a main hydraulic pump unit along with brackets.
Specifications
  • Hydraulic Motor - 100cc/rev
  • Pull - 750Kg
  • Speed - 75M/min
  • Oil flow - 27 L/min
  • Body Materials - Marine grade Aluminum, Stainless steel & Galvanized parts
  • Gross Weight - 200Kg
  • Pump Unit - 30 cc/rev (Pump will be driven by a coupling that connects to the vessel's main engine.)
  • Oil Tank Capacity - 35 Liters
