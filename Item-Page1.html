Model

Di12062011

Description

We offer this high-quality hauler which is offered on par with international standards and is built for medium and commercial purse seine fishing. The hauler is a very highly effective fishing winch used in commercial fishing that had proven to be of high quality when incorporated with fishing boats operating in Sri Lanka.
It is manufactured with a heavy stainless steel or brass structure, brass sleeves, a gypsy wheel, and idler rollers. The control valve attached to the winch body provides easy operation of the winch, which is essential for effectiveness.
It includes a main hauling unit with a control valve, side rollers, hydraulic hoses, and a main hydraulic pump unit with brackets. It comprises an oil tank & pressure relief valve. This equipment can help your global fishing company to effectively undertake commercial fishing.
Specifications
  • Oil Flow - 50 LPM
  • Pressure - 175 Bar
  • Sleeve line pull - 1250 kg
  • Gypsy Diameter - 20 cm
  • Gypsy Line Speed - 0-35 M/min
  • Gross Weight - 270kg
  • Body Material - Stainless steel, Brass, Galvanized brackets
  • Pump Unit - 50 cc/rev (Pump will be driven by a coupling that connects to the vessel's main engine.)
  • Speed - 0 -70 EPM
  • Rope Size - 6-18 mm
  • Sleeve Diameter - 41 cm
  • Sleeve line Speed - 0-70 M/min
  • Gypsy Line Pull - 1750 kg
  • Hydraulic Motor - OMSY 200 (Connected to sleeves through a heavy hub with a stainless shaft and roller baring)
